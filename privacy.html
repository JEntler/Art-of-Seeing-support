About this policy

This policy explains the Mac app, this website, and voluntary support contact separately.

The app on your Mac

The app works offline.

No account. No subscription. No tracking.

The app stores preferences and custom presets on your Mac. New activity history is not recorded. Existing app-activity totals from earlier versions remain local under the 90-day retention limit, applied when the app updates or restores saved state. It does not record what is on your screen, your browsing, your gaze, or health measurements.

No reminder data is sent to an app-operated server. Launch at Login is optional and controlled through macOS.

There are no advertising, analytics, remote crash-reporting, cloud-sync, or account services in the app. Opening an external help link opens your default browser and is subject to that destination's privacy practices.

Diagnostics you choose to share

You may create a local diagnostic export to help troubleshoot. Review it before sharing. The app does not automatically upload it. A support message can include information you deliberately provide, such as app and macOS versions, relevant settings, a description, or an attachment.

Apple services

Apple handles App Store purchases, distribution, and any system diagnostics you choose to share with Apple under its own policies. The app developer does not receive your payment details through this app. See Apple's privacy information.

This website

These static pages contain no analytics scripts, advertising pixels, forms, third-party fonts, or marketing cookies. This site uses GitHub Pages. GitHub processes requests to deliver and protect the site, including IP address logging. See GitHub Pages data collection and GitHub's privacy statement.

We do not claim that the hosting provider records no IP addresses or other request information.

Support and privacy requests

When you voluntarily contact support, the recipient receives your message and any details or attachments you send. They are used to respond and troubleshoot. Please do not send health information, passwords, financial details, or private diagnostic files.

Changes
