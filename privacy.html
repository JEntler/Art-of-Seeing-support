About this policy

This policy explains the Mac app, this website, and voluntary support contact separately. The legal provider identity remains unconfirmed in this local preview.

The app on your Mac

The app works offline.

No account. No subscription. No tracking.

The app stores preferences and custom presets on your Mac. Local history is optional and off by default. When enabled, it stores app actions and timer outcomes for at most 90 days. It does not record what is on your screen, your browsing, your gaze, or health measurements. You can stop new recording and clear existing history in Settings.

Local notification requests are handled by macOS. No reminder data is sent to an app-operated server. Launch at Login is optional and controlled through macOS.

There are no advertising, analytics, remote crash-reporting, cloud-sync, or account services in the app. Opening an external help link opens your default browser and is subject to that destination's privacy practices.

Diagnostics you choose to share

You may create a local diagnostic export to help troubleshoot. Review it before sharing. The app does not automatically upload it. A support message can include information you deliberately provide, such as app and macOS versions, relevant settings, a description, or an attachment.

Apple services

Apple handles App Store purchases, distribution, and any system diagnostics you choose to share with Apple under its own policies. The app developer does not receive your payment details through this app. See Apple's privacy information.

This website

These static pages contain no analytics scripts, advertising pixels, forms, third-party fonts, or marketing cookies. Hosting has not been selected for this local preview. Before publication, this paragraph must identify the approved host and link to its data-handling policy.

We do not claim that the hosting provider records no IP addresses or other request information.

Support and privacy requests

When you voluntarily contact support, the recipient receives your message and any details or attachments you send. They are used to respond and troubleshoot. Please do not send health information, passwords, financial details, or private diagnostic files.

This local preview has no approved public support contact yet. The contact route must be confirmed before this site is published.

If a public issue tracker is offered, posts are public and may require a provider account. Review that provider's policy before posting. Ask through the approved contact route about access to or deletion of support correspondence; no response-time or retention guarantee is asserted here.

Changes

This page will identify its revision date when the product or processing changes. A new network, analytics, or cloud feature would require a separate product and privacy review.
