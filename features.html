Features

A rhythm that fits your day.

A clear countdown, a gentle prompt, and simple controls.

Right in your menu bar.

Choose Start to begin a work interval. The countdown at the top tells you when your break is due. Stop keeps reminders inactive until you start again.

The eye is open while active and closed while stopped, paused, or waiting for your return. Option-click starts or stops reminders; right-click opens quick controls.

A preset of your own.

Start with 20–20–20, Gentle, or Focus. Duplicate a preset or create one with your own interval and activities. Settings save automatically.

Work intervals range from 10 to 120 minutes. Ordinary breaks range from 20 seconds to 5 minutes, with longer multi-step routines available.

A moment away.

A small break window shows the activity. Start when ready or skip. Take a Break Now begins your selected routine immediately. Finish Early ends a running break; I’m Back begins the next work interval.

Use Previous and Next in a routine with multiple activities. Choose whether the window remembers its position or opens in the center.

Your control, on your Mac.

Choose a preset, reset the timer, or pause whenever you need to.

Break history is not recorded. Preferences stay on your Mac.

The app works offline.
