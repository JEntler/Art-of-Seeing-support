Frequently asked questions

A little clarity.

The everyday details, in plain language.

How do I start reminders?

Open the app and click its eye in the menu bar, then choose Start Reminders. Opening the menu alone does not start or reset a timer.

What do the eye states mean?

Open: a work interval is running. Closed: paused, waiting for a schedule, or temporarily unavailable. A marked eye means a break is ready or running, or a delivery issue needs attention. The popover explains the state in text.

Does Reset Timer also resume?

No. Reset starts a fresh interval while preserving your enabled or manually paused choice. It does not award a finished break.

Why did I not see a notification?

Allow notifications in macOS System Settings → Notifications. Focus and your notification settings can suppress banners. The menu-bar state remains available; the app does not bypass macOS settings.

What happens if I ignore a reminder?

It stays actionable for five minutes, without repeated nagging. Then the cycle continues on its normal cadence. Optional history calls it unanswered, never completed.

What is the difference between Pause and Quit?

Pause keeps the app open with reminders suspended. Quit closes the app and cancels its owned reminders. A crash can leave one already scheduled OS notification until it is removed or expires; the app removes stale notifications on relaunch.

Can I choose work hours?

Yes. Schedules and quiet hours are optional. A manual pause wins over the schedule. A manual break can run outside work hours without enabling recurring reminders.

Does Launch at Login start the timer?

It opens the app. Starting reminders on launch is a separate preference. Both begin off. macOS may require approval for a login item.

How do I delete my history?

Open Settings → History, choose Delete All History…, then confirm Delete History. History is optional and stored locally. Turning Record local history off and choosing Save Changes stops recording but keeps existing records until you delete them or retention prunes them.

Is the price a subscription?

The planned US launch price is $7.99 paid upfront. There is no subscription or in-app purchase. The Mac App Store will show the final local price and any applicable taxes. No future iOS app or every future major version is promised as part of this purchase.

Is this an eyesight treatment?

A screen-break and habit-support tool, not an eyesight test or medical treatment. It does not measure or correct vision.

Seek professional care for persistent vision changes. Seek urgent eye care for sudden vision loss, severe eye pain, a sudden increase in floaters or flashes, or a curtain or shadow across your vision.

Is there an iPhone or iPad app?

There is no shipping iOS companion. This website describes the Mac app; no iOS release date or purchase entitlement is promised.

How do I uninstall it?

Quit from the menu-bar menu, then move the app from Applications to the Trash. Disable Launch at Login first if enabled. Delete local history in Settings first if you want to remove it; deleting an app does not necessarily delete its local container data.
