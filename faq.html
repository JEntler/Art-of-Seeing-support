Frequently asked questions

A little clarity.

The everyday details, in plain language.

How do I start reminders?

Click the eye in your menu bar, then choose Start. Stop keeps reminders inactive until you start again. Opening the menu does not start or reset a timer.

What does the eye mean?

Open means reminders are active. Closed means stopped, paused, or waiting for your return. Open the menu to read the countdown or current break state. General can also show the remaining time beside the eye, including during a timed pause, using h for hours, m for minutes, and s for seconds.

Can I start a break before the timer ends?

Yes. Choose Take a Break Now in the menu or by right-clicking the menu-bar eye. It begins your selected routine without turning stopped recurring reminders on.

Can the break window remember its position?

Yes. In Settings → General, choose Remember Last Position or Always in the Center. A remembered position survives relaunch; if its display is unavailable, the window opens in the center.

Does Reset Timer also resume?

No. Reset begins a fresh interval while preserving your active or stopped choice.

What happens if I ignore a reminder?

It stays actionable for five minutes without repeated prompting. Then the cycle continues. An unanswered reminder is never treated as a completed break.

What is the difference between Stop, Pause, and Quit?

Stop keeps reminders inactive until you choose Start. Pause resumes after 30 minutes, one hour, or at the next local midnight. Quit closes the app and stops reminders. It reads Quit & Stop while reminders or a break are active, and Quit Art of Seeing when inactive or paused.

Does Launch at Login start the timer?

It opens the app. Starting the last preset on launch is a separate preference. Both begin off. macOS may require approval for the login item.

Does the app record activity history?

No new activity history is recorded. Existing totals from earlier versions stay on your Mac under the 90-day retention limit, applied when the app updates or restores saved state.

Is the price a subscription?

The planned US launch price is $7.99 paid upfront. There is no subscription or in-app purchase. The Mac App Store shows local prices and applicable taxes. No future iOS app or every future major version is promised as part of the purchase.

Is this an eyesight treatment?

A screen-break and habit-support tool, not an eyesight test or medical treatment. It does not measure or correct vision.

Is there an iPhone or iPad app?

There is no shipping iOS companion. This website describes the Mac app; no iOS release date or purchase entitlement is promised.

How do I uninstall it?

Disable Launch at Login if enabled, quit the app, then move it from Applications to the Trash. Deleting an app does not necessarily delete its local container data.
