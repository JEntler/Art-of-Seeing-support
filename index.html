Start and stop reminders

Click the eye in your menu bar, choose a preset, then click Start. The countdown tells you when your next break is due. Stop leaves reminders inactive; Reset Timer starts a fresh interval. Pause offers 30 minutes, one hour, or until the next local midnight. Quitting Art of Seeing stops reminders.

Take a break

When a break is ready, choose Start Break or Skip. Take a Break Now starts your routine immediately. Finish Early ends a running break; I’m Back starts the next work interval after a finished break. In the app’s Guide, use Try beside an activity to try it on its own. See the guide for the controls and activities.

Make it yours

Choose 20–20–20, Gentle, or Focus, or create a routine in Settings → Presets. General contains break-window position, preparation, sound, and return behavior. Launch at Login and starting the last preset on launch are separate choices, both off by default. Settings save automatically.

Use a keyboard shortcut or Shortcuts

Choose a global shortcut in Settings → Automation to start or stop from another app. None is assigned by default, and no Accessibility permission is needed. Open Shortcuts from the same pane to use Start Reminders, Stop Reminders, Reset Timer, or Get Status.

For troubleshooting, include your macOS version, Art of Seeing version, and what happened. Export Redacted Diagnostics is in Settings → Advanced. Review the file before attaching it; please don’t send passwords, health information, or unrelated private files.

The app

The app works offline. There are no accounts, analytics, advertising, cloud sync, or remote crash reports. Preferences and custom presets stay on your Mac. No new break history is recorded; older local totals retain their 90-day limit.

Diagnostics are exported only when you ask and are never uploaded by the app. Links open in your browser or email app. Apple handles purchases and any system diagnostics you choose to share with Apple under its own policies.

This website

These static pages have no scripts, tracking pixels, forms, or third-party fonts. This site uses GitHub Pages. GitHub processes requests to deliver and protect the site, including IP address logging. See GitHub Pages data collection and GitHub's privacy statement.

When you contact support

If you email support, we receive your address, message, and attachments to respond to your request. For privacy questions or to request deletion of support correspondence, contact help@sample.audio. The full policy explains local data and separate services.
