The guide

Art of Seeing Guide

Simple controls for regular screen breaks.

Start and Stop

Click the eye in your menu bar and choose Start. The countdown appears beneath the app name. Stop leaves reminders inactive until you choose Start again; your settings stay saved.

The default 20–20–20 preset offers a 20-second Distance Look break after 20 minutes. Choose Gentle, Focus, or create your own in Settings → Presets. Use Duplicate to start from an existing preset.

Pause, reset, and return

Reset Timer
Begins a fresh work interval without resuming a stopped timer. Opening the menu never resets the countdown.
Pause
Choose 30 minutes, one hour, or until the next local midnight. The top of the menu shows when reminders resume. The optional time beside the eye uses h, m, or s.
Take a Break Now
Begins your selected routine immediately from the menu or the menu-bar eye’s right-click menu. It does not turn recurring reminders on if they were stopped.
Start Break
Begins the guided timer when a break is ready.
Skip
Dismisses a ready reminder and begins a fresh work interval.
Previous / Next
Move between activities in a routine with more than one activity.
Finish Early / I’m Back
Finish Early ends a running break. Choose I’m Back when you are ready to begin the next work interval.
Quit
Quit & Stop appears while reminders or a break are active; Quit Art of Seeing appears when inactive or paused. Both close the app and stop reminders. No background helper keeps reminding you.

Ignored reminders are unanswered, never completed breaks.

An unanswered reminder expires after five minutes and the work cycle continues. Display sleep suspends reminders; after a longer absence, the timer starts fresh.

Make it yours

Settings save automatically. In General, choose whether break windows remember their last position or open in the center. Launch at Login and starting the last preset on launch are separate preferences. General also contains preparation, sound, and whether to wait for I’m Back. The introduction stays visible until dismissed and can be restored from General.

In Automation, choose an optional global shortcut or open Apple’s Shortcuts app. Start Reminders, Stop Reminders, Reset Timer, and Get Status use the same controls as the menu bar.

Activities

In the app’s Guide, choose Try beside an activity to try it on its own. This does not turn stopped recurring reminders on.

Distance Look

Look away from your screen at something comfortably distant—about 20 ft / 6 m away when practical. Let your eyes rest; don't strain for detail.

Gentle Blinks

Blink slowly and completely a few times. Keep it gentle.

Eyes Closed

Let your eyes rest gently closed.

Look Around

Let your attention move naturally around your surroundings. No staring or forcing detail.

Step Away

Step away from the screen for a few minutes.

Optional activity; not part of the default routine.

Viewing Distance

Look comfortably at a nearby object, then something farther away. Keep it easy and stop if uncomfortable.

Optional activity; not part of the default routine.

No distant view? Gently close your eyes for this break. An image on your monitor is not a distant view.

Use with care

A screen-break and habit-support tool, not an eyesight test or medical treatment. It does not measure or correct vision.

Perform activities only when safe. Stop if an activity causes discomfort. Never look at the sun or an intense light, or apply pressure to your eyes.

Seek professional care for persistent vision changes. Seek urgent eye care for sudden vision loss, severe eye pain, a sudden increase in floaters or flashes, or a curtain or shadow across your vision.

About the reminder pattern

20–20–20 is a familiar reminder pattern, not a proven universal dose. Research findings differ, and this app has not been clinically tested.

See the 2023 study of 20–20–20 reminders and 2023 experiment on break timing.
