This website

The site uses semantic landmarks and headings, a skip link, visible keyboard focus, descriptive screenshot text, and native expandable FAQ controls. It has no animated content, autoplay, or interactions that require a pointer. Text reflows on narrow screens.

We test the built pages for missing alt text and broken internal links. Actual keyboard, zoom, contrast, and screen-reader observations are recorded during release QA. This is not a claim of formal accessibility certification.

The Mac app

States are explained in text as well as artwork. Instructions appear before a break so you can look away. Sounds are optional. A finished guided timer is not described as measured eye behavior.

The app uses native controls with accessibility labels and conventional keyboard actions. Platform accessibility declarations remain subject to testing on the final signed build; no blanket VoiceOver or all-display compatibility guarantee is made.

Share an obstacle.

If a control or page is difficult to use, share the app or page, your macOS/browser version, and the assistive technology or setting involved if you wish. No health details are needed.
