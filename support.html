The guide explains timers, schedules, and activities. The FAQ covers notifications, Launch at Login, history, and uninstalling.

Get in touch.

This local preview has no approved public support contact yet. The contact route must be confirmed before this site is published.

Include your macOS version, app version, what you expected, and what happened. If possible, list the steps that reproduce it. Please review any screenshot or local diagnostic export before sharing.

Do not send passwords, financial information, health information, account identifiers, or unrelated private files. The app never uploads diagnostics automatically.

Useful first checks.

  1. Open the menu-bar eye and read the current state.
  2. If paused or outside work hours, review the return time or schedule.
  3. For missing banners, check macOS System Settings → Notifications and your Focus settings.
  4. Try Take a Break Now to use a short guided timer without waiting a work interval.
  5. Quit and reopen the app if controls stop responding. Old notifications do not create completed breaks.

Eye-care questions

A screen-break and habit-support tool, not an eyesight test or medical treatment. It does not measure or correct vision.

Seek professional care for persistent vision changes. Seek urgent eye care for sudden vision loss, severe eye pain, a sudden increase in floaters or flashes, or a curtain or shadow across your vision.
