Purpose and safe use

A screen-break and habit-support tool, not an eyesight test or medical treatment. It does not measure or correct vision.

Seek professional care for persistent vision changes. Seek urgent eye care for sudden vision loss, severe eye pain, a sudden increase in floaters or flashes, or a curtain or shadow across your vision.

Never look directly at the sun or an intense light during a break.

Choose reminders suitable for your work and surroundings. Stop an activity if uncomfortable. The app cannot verify that you looked away or completed a physical activity, and it cannot replace professional eye care.

System limits

Quit closes the app and stops reminders. A reminder window does not verify that you saw it or performed the activity.

Documented display and session signals support suspension and return behavior. The app does not inspect private lock or Focus state, keep the computer awake, or block access to other apps.
